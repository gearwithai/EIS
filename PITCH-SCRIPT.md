# Pitch Script — Bryton Roofs Discovery Call
Confident, specific, not salesy. Everything references verified research.

---

## 90-second opener (read aloud)

> "Will — before we jump in, I did some homework on Bryton, and a few things stood out.
>
> First, you've got something almost no contractor has: a **perfect 5.0 with zero complaints, twenty years in, GAF-certified and running on license #87794.** I looked at the rest of the Greenville market — Roofing Solutions has 174 reviews, others lean on a 40-year story — but *nobody* in Eastern NC is pairing a flawless record with a modern web experience. That's an open lane, and it's yours to take.
>
> Second, here's the gap: that perfect reputation is basically **invisible on your own site.** It lives on Google and Yelp, so every homeowner has to leave brytonroofs.com to confirm you're the real deal. And every path on the site is 'request a free consultation' — which means when someone's got a leak at 11pm in a storm, they wait for a callback instead of taking action. That's leads cooling off.
>
> So I built you something to react to. It's a landing page concept where a homeowner gets a **real ballpark roof price in about 60 seconds** — no roofer in your market does that — and where your 5.0, your GAF certification, your 10-year warranty, and your insurance-claim help all do the selling before the phone ever rings. I'd rather show you than describe it. Can I share my screen?"

*(~90 seconds at a natural pace.)*

---

## 5 discovery questions (surface the pain so the page sells itself)

1. **Lead source & friction:** "When a new customer finds you today, what's the usual path — phone, the website form, a referral? And roughly how many website leads turn into actual jobs versus how many you'd want?"
   *Surfaces: form-only friction and conversion leakage.*

2. **After-hours / storm capture:** "When a storm rolls through Eastern NC and the phone blows up after hours, what happens to the calls you can't answer in the moment?"
   *Surfaces: missed urgency revenue → justifies instant-quote + 24/7 widget.*

3. **The pricing conversation:** "How much of your time goes into early 'what'll this cost' and financing questions before someone's even a real lead?"
   *Surfaces: estimator + financing calculator value.*

4. **Proof & reputation:** "You've got a flawless 5.0 and zero complaints — that's rare. Is anything you're doing today actually putting that in front of people before they call?"
   *Surfaces: the invisible-trust gap → reviews wall.*

5. **Growth target:** "If we turned the site into a machine that booked inspections while you're on a roof, what would 'a win' look like in the next 90 days — more residential replacements, more commercial, more insurance-claim work?"
   *Surfaces: the metric to design toward and frames the premium build as ROI.*

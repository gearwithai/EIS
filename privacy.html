Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

GearWithAI (“GearWithAI”, “we”, “us”) provides AI automation and website design services for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at sunny@gearwithai.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a demo or contact us, we collect details such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, and the message you send.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we may collect basic technical data — such as device type, browser, and pages visited — to keep the site secure and improve performance.
  • Cookies. We use only the cookies necessary to run the site and understand aggregate usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the demo or services you request.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.
  • To communicate with you about your request and, where you have opted in, relevant updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, hosting or communication tools), under agreements that protect your data, or when required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described here, or as required by law, after which we securely delete or anonymize it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email sunny@gearwithai.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work hard to safeguard your data and limit access to those who need it.

8. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version here with an updated date, and material changes will be made clear.

11. Contact
